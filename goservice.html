B2B Go Service

Optimised design. Smarter systems. Better results

Let’s Map Your Digital Growth

B2B Go Service : is a business-focused service platform designed to simplify everyday facility maintenance.

Industry:Home Services & Maintenance

It connects businesses with reliable plumbing, electrical, and carpentry services through a convenient subscription-based model.

Brand Story

Go Service is a modern home and business service platform built to make everyday maintenance simpler. From plumbing and electrical work to carpentry and other essential services, Go Service connects customers with professional assistance at their doorstep. With a focus on convenience, reliability, and quality service, the brand is creating a smarter and more accessible way to manage everyday service needs.

The Opportunity

Go Service had an opportunity to transform its existing digital presence into a modern, user-friendly service ecosystem. The goal was to redesign the website from scratch and introduce a dedicated mobile app that would make service discovery easier, improve customer convenience, and create a stronger digital foundation for future growth.

What We Delivered

Website Development

Built a responsive and scalable website designed to provide a seamless experience across devices.

Service Discovery

Organised multiple services into a clear structure so customers can find what they need faster.

User-Centric UI/UX

Simplified the customer journey with intuitive layouts, clear service categories, and easy navigation.

Custom Mobile App

Developed a user-friendly app that makes discovering and accessing services effortless.

Performance & Usability

Focused on smoother interactions, faster access, and a frictionless overall user experience.

Mobile-First Experience

Optimised the digital experience for smartphone users with a clean and responsive interface.
