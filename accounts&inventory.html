Inventory & Accounting Software

Manage Your Stock. Keep Your Accounts Connected

We developed a customized inventory and accounting software solution to help businesses manage products, purchases, sales, payments, and financial records through one integrated system.

From stock movement to financial tracking — designed around the way the business works.

One System for Inventory & Financial Management

The software was developed to connect everyday inventory activities with accounting processes, reducing the need to manage information across separate records.

Product & category management

Organize products, manage categories, update pricing, track availability, and maintain accurate product information for efficient business operations.

Stock in & stock out

Track incoming and outgoing inventory efficiently, monitor stock levels, prevent shortages, and maintain accurate records for smooth business operations.

Purchase & sales management

Efficiently manage purchases, sales transactions, invoices, stock levels, and payments to streamline business operations and maintain accurate records.

Supplier & customer management

Easily manage supplier and customer details, track transactions, monitor payments, and maintain strong business relationships efficiently.

Invoice & billing management

Generate and manage invoices, track billing records, monitor customer payments, and maintain organized transaction details for accurate accounting.

Income & expense tracking

Monitor earnings, record spending, manage budgets, and maintain accurate financial records for better financial control.

Stock and transaction history

Track stock levels and maintain detailed records of transactions, including sales, purchases, returns, and adjustments for accurate inventory management.

Payment Balance Tracking

Track received payments, pending dues, and outstanding balances to maintain accurate financial records and ensure timely collections.

Inventory & financial reports

Track stock levels, monitor expenses, and generate financial reports to support accurate accounting and better business decisions.

Cost control Management

Monitor expenses, reduce unnecessary spending, manage budgets, and improve profitability through efficient cost tracking and financial planning.

Where Inventory Meets Accounting

Integrated Stock & Sales Tracking Monitor stock movements, track sales, and maintain accurate inventory records in one centralized system.
Billing & Payment Management Generate bills, record payments, and manage transactions efficiently while keeping financial records organized.
Customer & Supplier Accounts Manage customer details, supplier information, outstanding balances, and transaction histories with ease.
